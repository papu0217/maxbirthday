Love & Faith 🙏

1 Corinthians 13:4-7

Love is patient, love is kind. It does not envy, it does not boast, it is not proud. It does not dishonor others, it is not self-seeking, it is not easily angered, it keeps no record of wrongs. Love does not delight in evil but rejoices with the truth. It always protects, always trusts, always hopes, always perseveres.

Tamil explanation: Anbu porumaiyanaadhu, anbu aruludaiyadhu. Anbu perumaiyatraadhu, veruppatraadhu. Anbu thannai naadiyaadhu, kobathil alariyadhu. Anbu thappai eppozhudhum ninaithukollaadhu; sathiyathil sandhosham adaiyum. Anbu ellathaiyum porukkum, ellathaiyum nambum, ellathaiyum nambikai kondirukkum, ellathaiyum thangaadhu.

Namma relationship la love means patience, kindness, trust and understanding. Sandai vandhaalum, misunderstanding vandhaalum, namma love eppovum strong ah irukanum. Naan unna patience ah, kindness ah, trust ah love panren my Gundus.

Ecclesiastes 4:9-10

Two are better than one, because they have a good return for their labor: If either of them falls down, one can help the other up.

Tamil explanation: Iruvar serndhu iruppadhu oruvarai vida nalladhu; avargal natpinal nanmai adaivar. Oruvar vizhundhaal, mattoruvar avana ezhuppugiraan.

Namma relationship la namma rendu perum oru team. Unaku kashtam vandha naan unna support panren; enaku kashtam vandha nee enna strong ah iruka vaippen. Namma rendu perum serndhu, life la ellathaiyum face pannalam.
